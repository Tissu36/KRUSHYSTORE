OG / early-season

Some early outfits later returned. For Renegade Raider and Aerial Assault Trooper, the Black & Gold style is the 2017-owner distinction; Skull Trooper’s Purple Glow and Ghoul Trooper’s Pink style are also original-owner styles.

Rare / exclusive

Here, “rare” refers to history or a limited unlock route, not the in-game colour tier. Shop rotations and availability can change, so check the item’s current status before relying on a rarity label.
